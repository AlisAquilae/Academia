# 🔎 Introduction
This course on hydrological processes deals with advanced topics in hydrological processes in catchments at various scales, such as processes and estimation methods for stream flow generation and groundwater (floods and drought development), deterministic and stochastic modelling approaches. The deterministic part deals with precipitation-runoff relationships using physical-mathematical and conceptual models (physically based approaches and reservoir models); examples of flood forecasting and hydrological drought analysis techniques (incl. drought propagation); determination of flow generating processes (quick flow, base flow, influence of groundwater system properties on discharge); determination of the effects of global change on stream flow generation and drought development. The stochastic part deals with black box models (in particular regression models). The course includes a field trip to Iceland, where the relation between hydro(geo)logy, physical catchment structure, climate and stream discharge in undisturbed catchments will be demonstrated and discussed. Impacts of floods and droughts will be elaborated.

The course is divided into 6 thematic modules:
1. Climate and the water cycle
2. Runoff and drought
3. Floods and lowlands
4. Variability and cryosphere
5. (Un)certainty
6. Synthesis and field excursion Iceland
