# 🔎 Introduction
This course delves into the theory and application of regional scale hydro(geo)logical models, offering a comprehensive understanding of their utility in managing water resources and addressing complex environmental challenges. As we enter an era of increasing climate variability and human-induced changes, there is an urgent need for sophisticated tools to assess and predict hydrological processes at a regional scale. Special attention will be paid to the modelling and coupling of surface water to groundwater systems, which usually operate at different temporal and spatial scales . Hydro(geo)logical processes covered in this course include: unsaturated (HYDRUS) and saturated groundwater (MODFLOW) flow and surface or open water flow (HEC-RAS). During practicals students will implement several modelling concepts in a relatively transparent programming environment (e.g., spreadsheets or the R-studio). They will get acquainted with a commercial software environment (GMS-MODFLOW) simulating different components of the hydrological cycle contained in different packages. Students will apply GMS-MODFLOW to real-world case studies (e.g., Wierden) and perform
parametric uncertainty analysis.

# 📖 Course Material
This course does not contain separate course material. Instead, all theory is presented in lectures and discussed during the practicals
