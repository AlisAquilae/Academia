# 🔎 Introduction
In the wake of recent catastrophic floods, such as those witnessed in Germany, Belgium and the Netherlands in July 2021, the importance of flood management has never been more pronounced. 

The course commences with a detailed examination of velocities, flow properties, and discharge in water systems. Students gain insights into the dynamic behaviour of water flow and its implications for flood management strategies. By elucidating the laws of conservation of mass, momentum, and energy, the course establishes a foundation for understanding the mechanics of steady water flow in conduits, rivers, and canals. Through rigorous analysis, students develop a clear comprehension of the underlying principles governing hydraulic systems. Emphasising hydrodynamics in rivers and streams, the course delves into advanced topics related to river flow dynamics. Students explore the intricacies of one-dimensional channel flow, deriving the St-Venant equations and mastering solution methods essential for analysing hydraulic phenomena.
