# 🔎 Introduction
During this advanced course we discuss and apply numerical techniques to study and solve problems in the realm of Earth sciences, such as hydrology, solute transport, hydraulics, meteorology and air quality. Topics that will be dealt with are: 
- Theory of numerical methods that are available to tackle hydrological and atmospheric flow problems
- Application of numerical methods on simplified systems 
- Implementation of simple simulation models 
- Sensitivity analysis on the model parameters in relation to model output 

The course is relevant for students that want to study and solve problems using numerical simulation models, to enhance process understanding or to support decision making.
