**Glacial Sediment Transport** refers to the transport of sediments by glaciers. As these glaciers move over their beds, they entrain and move material of all sizes. It is one of several ways for sediment transport to take place, alongside
- [[Fluvial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Aeolian Sediment Transport]]
- [[Coastal Sediment Transport]]
- [[Hillslope Sediment Transport]]
