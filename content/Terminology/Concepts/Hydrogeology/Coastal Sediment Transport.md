**Coastal Sediment Transport** refers to the transport of sediments due to the motions of waves and currents, taking place in near-shore environments. It is one of several ways for sediment transport to take place, alongside
- [[Fluvial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Aeolian Sediment Transport]]
- [[Glacial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Hillslope Sediment Transport]]
