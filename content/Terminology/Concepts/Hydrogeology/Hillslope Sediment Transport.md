**Hillslope Sediment Transport** refers to the movement of sediments (usually [[Regolith]]) downhill. This occurs through various methods, such as landslides, tree throw and [[Soil Creep]]. It is one of several ways for sediment transport to take place, alongside
- [[Fluvial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Aeolian Sediment Transport]]
- [[Coastal Sediment Transport]]
- [[Aeolian Sediment Transport]]
