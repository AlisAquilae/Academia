**Fluvial** sediment transport refers to the transport of sediment by rivers and streams, and the deposition of these sediments as well as landforms created by them. It is one of several ways in which sediments are transported:
- [[Aeolian Sediment Transport]]
- [[Coastal Sediment Transport]]
- [[Glacial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Hillslope Sediment Transport]]
