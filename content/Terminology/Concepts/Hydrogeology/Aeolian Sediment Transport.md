**Aeolian Sediment Transport** refers to the transport of sediments by wind. This process results in the formation of ripples and sand dunes. It is one of several ways to transport sediments, alongside
- [[Fluvial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Coastal Sediment Transport]]
- [[Glacial Sediment Transport]]
- [[Hillslope Sediment Transport]]
