The **Vapour pressure deficit** (VPD) refers to the difference between water vapour pressure at saturation ($e^* = f(T)$) and the actual water vapour pressure ($e$) of the near-surface air. 

>[!Important]
>The VPD is the driving force behind [[Evapotranspiration]]
