The **Specific humidity**, $q$, refers to the ratio between the mass of water vapour and the mass of dry air. It is often used to express the humidity deficit in air masses, as it has the advantage that it does not change with air density:

$$
\Delta q = q^* (T) - q
$$

in which $q^* (T)$ is the value of $q$ at saturation
