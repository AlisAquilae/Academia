The difference between a solid and a fluid can be introduced from a comparison between pudding. When tilted, pudding will deform over a fixed distance. In more formal terms, it reacts to a [[Shear force]] with a fixed deformation. It is therefore a solid. A **Fluid**, on the other hand, reacts to shear force with a continuous deformation, at a rate referred to as the [[Rate of strain]]. 

Fluids have 3 key properties
1. [[Fluid viscosity]]
2. [[Fluid density]]
3. [[Fluid surface tension]]
