**Dynamic Viscosity**, $\mu$, refers to a fluid's resistance to flow under force. It is measured in pressure (e.g. Pascal) times a measure of time (e.g. seconds). It is important, among others, in determining the [[Shear Strain]] in relation to the [[Shear Force|Shear Stress]].
