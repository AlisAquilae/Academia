The **Viscosity** of a [[Fluid]] refers to the "syrupiness" of that fluid. Or, in more formal terms, that fluid's property to resist [[Shear Strain]]. It is one of the three key properties of fluid, along with [[Fluid Density]] and [[Fluid Surface Tension]].

Viscosity can be further divided into [[Dynamic Viscosity]] and [[Kinematic Viscosity]].
