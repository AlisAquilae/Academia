The **Rate of Strain**, in the broadest sense, refers to the deformation of an object. In the context of solids, the rate of strain captures how quickly a solid deforms under stress. It is often associated with creep, plastic deformation, or viscoelastic behaviour (i.e., how a solid deforms over time under stress).

In a [[Fluid]], the rate of strain refers to a continuous deformation over time, since fluids do not have a fixed shape.
