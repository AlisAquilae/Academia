The **Rate of Strain**, $\gamma$ or $\varepsilon$, in the broadest sense, refers to the deformation of an object. In the context of solids, the rate of strain captures how quickly a solid deforms under stress. It is often associated with creep, plastic deformation, or viscoelastic behaviour (i.e., how a solid deforms over time under stress). In solids, the rate of strain has a rather niche use, and we more often use the strain itself. In a [[Fluid]], on the other hand, the rate of strain is more interesting. 

Depending on the force exerted, the rate of strain has different names
- In the context of [[Shear Force]], the rate of strain refers towards the [[Shear Rate]]
