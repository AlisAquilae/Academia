**Shear force**, sometimes referred to as **Shear stress**, is a force that acts tangentially to a surface, causing one layer of a material (such as a fluid) to slide past another. In simple terms, imagine rubbing your hands together—the frictional force that makes one hand move against the other is similar to shear stress in a fluid.

The shear force per unit area is denoted using $\tau$. 

The deformation in a fluid caused by a shearing force $\overrightarrow{F}$  which acts tangentially to the surface to which it is applied is caused the [[Shear Strain]]
