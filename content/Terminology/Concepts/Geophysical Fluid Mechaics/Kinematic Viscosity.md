**Kinematic Viscosity**, $v$, refers to how "fluid" something is when gravity is the only force acting upon that fluid. It is the ratio of the [[Dynamic Viscosity]] and the [[Fluid Density]]. 

$$
v = \frac{\mu}{\rho}
$$
