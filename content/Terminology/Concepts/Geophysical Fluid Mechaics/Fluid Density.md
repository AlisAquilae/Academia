**Fluid Density**, $\rho$, is equivalent to the [[Specific Mass]] or mass per unit of volume of a [[Fluid]]. It is one of the three key characteristics of fluids, alongside [[Fluid Viscosity]] and [[Fluid Surface Tension]].
