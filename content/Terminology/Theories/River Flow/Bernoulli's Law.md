**Bernoulli's Law** refers to the Law of conservation of energy. 

For the case of fluids, we distinguish three different forms of energy for fluids:
1. Potential energy
2. Kinetic energy
3. Pressure energy

This is not an exhaustive list of energies that can be considered. Electromagnetic energy is another form of energy, but is not relevant for flowing water.
