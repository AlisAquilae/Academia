**Newton's Law of Viscosity** states that the [[Shear Force|Shear Stress]] of a fluid is proportional to that fluid's [[Shear Rate]], in the following relation:

$$
\tau = \mu \frac{\mathrm du}{\mathrm dy}
$$
where
- $\tau$ is the shear stress
- $\mu$ is the [[Dynamic Viscosity]]

Later, scientists found that not all fluids follow this relation. Fluids which do are called [[Newtonian Fluids]]. Most fluids are Newtonian, including water and air. An example of a fluid which is not Newtonian is quicksand: here, $\mu$ increases with the rate of shear.
