**Bernoulli's equation**  states that as far as the effects of viscosity can be neglected, the sum of kinetic energy, pressure energy and potential energy remains constant along a streamline. 

$$
\frac{u_i u_i}{2} + \frac{p}{\rho} + gh = C \; : \; \text{constant along a streamline}
$$
