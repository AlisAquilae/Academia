# St-Venant equations
It is all about bookkeeping what comes in and what goes out. Not everything which comes in has to go out, and the other way around. For example evaporation. Variation in space and time can also be due to bed resistance

We will try to set up a mass balance and a momentum balance. 

Upstream inflow - downstream outflow + external in/outflow = change in storage. Goes for mass, momentum, energy, etc. Any preserved quantity.
