# Intro
Recap of previous lecture
- Parametrisation: lump all small processes together. 
- Water depth relative to deepest level of river (a(t,x)). h(t,x) relative to some datum, e.g. NAP.
- Non-physical ways of modeling better done by observations and regression modelling. However, this does not hold for processes not captured by current or historic observations
- x-direction sometimes referred to as s-direction, for streamwise. This is because x is not a cartesian coordinate.
