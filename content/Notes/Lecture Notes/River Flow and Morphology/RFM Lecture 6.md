# Drag forces
We typically have a scale of an object perpendicular to the flow (opposed to flow direction) has 100% form drag and 0% friction drag

An object parallel to the flow is 0% form drag and 100% friction drag

friction drag = viscous drag, and form drag = pressure drag

In front of the obstacle, flow speed decreases and therefore force increases (Bernouli's law). After, speeds up again and more force. The total force is drag.

We can mathematically decompose the drag force into these two components. 

Drag component is horizontal force, vertical component is the lift force.
