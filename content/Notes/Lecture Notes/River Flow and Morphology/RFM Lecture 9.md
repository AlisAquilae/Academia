# Dunes definition
Dunes are also present underwater, sub-aqueous dunes. 

A bedform is a feature that
- Develops on the interface of a flow and a movable bed
- And is the result of bed material being moved by this flow

We will look at unidirectional bedforms (e.g. river flows). But you could also think of estuary or sea bedforms, which are affected by multiple directions. 

Implications of bedform dynamics
- Determine flow resistance, influence flood management
- Influence river bed stability. 
- Influence sediment transport. 
- Influence navigability

# Bedform classification
Lower-stage plane bed
- Flow velocity is not sufficient to form bedforms. Little bedload transport. 

Ripples:
- Slight bedload transport
- Evenly spaced, cm-scale, fine sand, subcritical flow
- Develop in the laminar/viscous sublayer
- Hydraulically smooth regime (grainsizes smaller than depth of lami)

Dunes
- More bedload transport

Upper-stage plane bed
- More sediment in suspension, little dunes

Antidunes
