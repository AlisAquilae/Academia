# Part 1
Q1: They have the same order of magnitude, and they are normally distributed between cases

Q2: When the head is fixed west, and the east head is increased, this is associated with a linear increase in the flux

Q3: If the k-field is heterogeneous, the boundary flux no longer increases linearly with the head. The boundary flux is no longer dependent on the head alone, but also on the (effective) k.

Q4: The geometric mean is best for spatially correlated simulations (in blobs)

Q5: The arithmetic mean is best for stratified conductivity fields (i.e. parallel flow, e.g. preferential flow paths)

Q6: The harmonic mean is best for stratified conductivity fields and vertical/serial flow
