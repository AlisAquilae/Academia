# Recap
We start with Darcy's law. 

$$
q = -K \frac{dh}{dx}
$$

$$
K = \frac{k \rho g}{\mu}
$$

- $k$ is intrinsic permeability
- $\rho$ is density
- $\mu$ is dynamic viscosity

This $K$ is hydraulic conductivity, which thus depends both on the fluid and the soil properties.
