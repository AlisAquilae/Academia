# Drought
Often 1976 mentioned in the Netherlands. In KNMI records, this was an extremely dry summer. Sparked relevance of drought research. A lot of initiatives, monitoring, field experiments, etc.

It took a few decades for another main drought to make the headlines: summer of 2003. Caused shockwaves throughout Europe. This drought was virtually impossible. Statistical estimation: once in 10000 years. However, not special if you place it in the ccontext of a future climate. In fact, would be a relatively cold summer. 

ET largest term in water balance during drought. Impact of soil moisture and land cover uncertain.
