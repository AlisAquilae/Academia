# Intro
Today: Nature-Based Solutions. This is an introduction to what we will be seeing and learning during the excursion

In 1953 watersnoodramp. This led to deltaworks

Dam as water on both sides, dike is in the edge of land. 

Coasts in the Netherlands have been fully engineered to satisfy human needs. Hard engineering approach: focus on keeping floods out and confining natural coastal dynamics to specific areas ('buitendijks')

Some issues with land vs. water approach: salinisation and seepage

Hard engineering is effective in short to medium flood prevention, but the dutch delta has become 'locked-in' - stuck behind grey infrastructure -> continuously raising the levees the only option. With ongoing sea-level rise, parts of the system already reached the limit (e.g. boils)

Nature-based solutions address societal challenges through actions to protect, sustainably manage, and restore natural and modified ecosystems, benefiting people and nature at the same time. 
- Solutions inspired by nature
- Address variety of societal challenges at once, instead of one at a time
- Resilient to change (e.g. sea level rise)
- Cost-effective, energy-sufficient
- Provide many ecosystem services

Sand-Engine: Mega-Nourishment Dutch Coast with sand
- Reinforcement of coast
- Minimal disturbance, based on natural dynamics
- Provides recreational value
- Expensive, but less than sand suppletion in dunes. 

NBS require a systems approach
- Understand the processes at the delta level
- Often monodisciplinary solutions create problems elsewhere in the system
- Create integrated, optimized solutions across disciplines
