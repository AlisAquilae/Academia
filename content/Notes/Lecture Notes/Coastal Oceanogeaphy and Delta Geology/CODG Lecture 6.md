# Intro
Today we talk about different methods. Each method has its own timescale and relevant research questions for which it is used

# ADCP
ADCP makes use of sound waves, sending them into the river channel, for example, and where and at what speed they come back. This can give a sense of how fast a river current is moving. This is a very short-term snapshot

# Marker Horizons
Again relatively short-term processes. Place something on the land, and see what happens. E.g. pvc pipe, ceramic tile, turf carpet, brick dust, etc.

# Geodetic Observations and SETs
Monitor vertical (subsidence)  and horizontal movements of tectonic plates

# Short-lived radioisotopes
The idea is that radiocative lead is naturally produced at a relatively constant rate in the atmosphere, giving an idea of sediment deposition and mixing up to approx 100 years
