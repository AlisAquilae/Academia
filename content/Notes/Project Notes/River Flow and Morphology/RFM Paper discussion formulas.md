$$
\overbrace{\overline{gW \frac{|u|u}{C^2}}}^{\text{Friction}} + \overbrace{\overline{gA \frac{\partial h}{\partial x}}}^{\text{Pressure gradient}} + \overbrace{\overline{\left( 2u \frac{\partial A}{\partial t} + u^2 \frac{\partial A}{\partial x} \right)}}^{\text{Acceleration}} \left|+\overbrace{\overline{\frac{\partial Au}{\partial t}}}^{\text{Storage}} + \overbrace{\overline{gA \frac{h \partial \rho}{\rho \partial x}}}^{\text{Density gradient}}\right. = 0
$$

Here:
- $g$ is gravity
- $W$ is the width
- $u$ is the velocity
- $C$ is the Chézy coefficient
- $A$ is the cross-sectional area
- $H$ is the height
- $x$ is the longitudinal distance
- $t$ is time
- $\rho$ is density


Assumptions:
1. One-directional
2. Tide-averaged


---

$$
\frac{s_o - s_f - \frac{2 \, u \, I}{g \, A}}{1 - \text{Fr}^2} = 0
$$


Here:
- $S_o$ is the bed slope, defined as $\frac{Q^2_e}{C^2 A^2_e R_e}$
- $S_f$ is the friction slope, defined as $\frac{Q^2}{C^2 A^2 R}$, i.e. equal to the bed slope
- $Fr$ is the Froude number, defined as $\sqrt{\frac{Q^2 \, b}{g \, A^3}}$

In all of these,
- $Q$ is the discharge
- $C$ is the Chézy coefficient
- $A$ is the wetted area, i.e. the cross-sectional area
- $R$ is the hydraulic radius (where $R_e$ refers to the hydraulic radius in a state of equilibrium)
- $b$ is the width
- $g$ is gravity
- $u$ is velocity
- $I$ is the intensity per unit of time and per unit of river length of the sum of all the external flows
